Remap any key. Build any macro.

A fast, native menu bar app for remapping keys on your Mac — from a simple WASD-to-arrow-keys swap to a multi-step keyboard shortcut macro, active globally or only in the apps you choose.

Download for Mac See features

No account. No cloud sync of your keystrokes. Runs entirely on your Mac.

Everything you need to make your keyboard yours

Simple remaps and full macros live side by side in the same lightweight app.

Simple key remaps

Swap WASD for the arrow keys, send Caps Lock to Control, or remap any key to any other key in a couple of clicks.

Full keyboard macros

Trigger a whole sequence of keystrokes from a single key press, with a custom delay between each step.

Per-app profiles

Set a mapping to run everywhere, or scope it to specific apps — a gaming profile that only activates in your game, for example.

Lives in your menu bar

No Dock icon, no clutter. Toggle remapping on or off and see what's active from a single click in the menu bar.

Everything stays local

Your key mappings are stored on disk on your Mac. Nothing is sent to a server, and there's no account to create.

Import & export profiles

Share a profile as a JSON file, or back one up before trying something new.

Set up a remap in under a minute

Everything happens in one settings window — no config files to hand-edit.

1

Create a profile

Name it, and choose whether it applies everywhere or only to specific apps.

2

Capture a trigger key

Click the key field and press the physical key you want to remap.

3

Choose remap or macro

Pick a single output key, or build a multi-step macro with per-step delays.

4

Turn it on

Enable the mapping and it takes effect immediately, system-wide.
